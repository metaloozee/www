# Backpack

Backpack is a self-hostable multi-model AI research and chat app: 6+ LLM providers, RAG research spaces, cross-chat memory, configurable MCP servers, and a versioned artifact workspace.

- **Type:** Open-source AI research and chat app
- **Stack:** TypeScript, Postgres, Redis, RAG, MCP
- **Years:** 2024 to 2025
- **Repo:** [metaloozee/backpack](https://github.com/metaloozee/backpack)

## Why I built it

I built Backpack to survive jumping into second-year engineering with a month until finals. It was the hardest thing I'd shipped yet.

It grew into an open-source NotebookLM-style tool with web and academic search, plus DeepResearch via [rsrch.tech](https://github.com/metaloozee/rsrch.tech).
