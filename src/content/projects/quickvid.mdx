# QuickVid

Summarize, verify, and chat with any YouTube video.

- **Type:** Web app
- **Stack:** Next.js, Neon, LangChain, Gemini
- **Year:** 2024
- **Status:** Shut down
- **Repo:** [metaloozee/quickvid](https://github.com/metaloozee/quickvid)

## How it works

I started with Whisper and GPT in 2022, then rebuilt around direct transcripts and Gemini: chunking long videos, embedding into a vector store, RAG chat, and internet-backed verification.

## Results

LangChain featured it. 173 GitHub stars, 15 forks, 100+ active users. Summarization went from minutes to about 10 seconds.

## Why it ended

YouTube ToS and crawler blocking made the workflow unsustainable, so I shut it down. Still the most successful side project I've shipped.
